02 · Explore

Plant Information

Botanical Identity


Botanical Name: Phoenix roebelenii O'Brien

Family: Arecaceae

The plant belongs to the date palm group and is widely valued as an ornamental palm.

Appearance & Characteristics


The Pygmy Date Palm is a relatively small palm with a slender trunk and soft, arching, feather-like leaves.


Its compact appearance makes it suitable for landscaping and container cultivation.

Common Uses


🌿 Garden decoration

🪴 Container planting

🏠 Indoor and outdoor landscaping

🌴 Tropical-style landscape design

03 · Cause & Effect

Effects of the Plant

🌿

Positive Effect

The plant adds greenery and an attractive tropical appearance to gardens and indoor spaces.

🏡

Environmental Value

As a living ornamental plant, it contributes to green spaces and improves the visual quality of landscapes.

⚠️

Possible Risk

The basal leaflets can form sharp structures, so care should be taken while handling or pruning the plant.

04 · Learn More

Plant Care & Problems

💧 Overwatering +
Excessive moisture can contribute to root problems. Good drainage is important for maintaining a healthy plant.
🐛 Pests +
Like other palms, the plant may experience problems with pests such as scale insects. Regular observation can help identify problems early.
❄️ Cold / Frost +
Cold and frost can damage the foliage. Protection may be necessary in colder climates.

🌿 Medicinal Use

The Pygmy Date Palm is primarily known as an ornamental and landscaping plant. There is no well-established medicinal use supported by the horticultural sources used for this project.

⚠️ Important: The plant should not be presented as a medicinal treatment without reliable scientific evidence. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before using any plant medicinally.
